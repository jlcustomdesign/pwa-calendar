# Calendar Rule: Freeze WeeklyCalendarCard

The `WeeklyCalendarCard.astro` component is the **official, locked calendar component** for the application.

## Strict Guidelines
- **DO NOT MODIFY** `src/components/features/dashboard/WeeklyCalendarCard.astro`'s layout, CSS, pill animations, horizontal scrolling, or transform math unless the user specifically and explicitly commands you to do so.
- The pill morphing, circle z-indexing, and expand/collapse transitions are delicately calibrated and easy to break.
- Treat this component as frozen and production-stable.
